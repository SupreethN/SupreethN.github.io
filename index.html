Supreeth Narasimhaswamy

Research Scientist, Luma AI · Physical AI & Robotics · Image & Video Generation


I am a Research Scientist at Luma AI, building multimodal foundation models for Physical AI and Robotics. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at Adobe, where I worked on image and video generation and editing for Adobe Firefly.

I received my PhD from CVLab at Stony Brook University, advised by Prof. Minh Hoai, where I built vision models and datasets to understand how human hands contact, interact with, and grasp objects.

Selected Work

Zero-real Agent Policy (ZAP)
Core contributor · Luma AI, 2026
A 27B open model trained only in simulation that beats state-of-the-art VLAs and matches GPT-6 Astra on a real bimanual robot, while being 3× faster and 17× cheaper per task, with zero real-world training data.
Adobe Firefly Image 4
Core contributor · Adobe, 2025
Adobe's foundation text-to-image generation model, trained on a commercially safe dataset.
Layered Image Editing and Decomposition
Core contributor · Adobe Firefly
Decomposing images into editable layers for generative editing. Users specify which objects to separate into layers, enabling precise, localized control over each object's attributes, size, and orientation.
Region Prompting in Adobe Firefly
Project lead · Adobe MAX, 2024
Prompting different regions of an image independently in Firefly, enabling control over the size and orientation of objects. Presented at Adobe MAX 2024.
Grasping Virtual Objects with Real Hands for Extended Reality
Inventor · Snap Research, US Patent
Enabling users to grasp and interact with virtual objects in extended reality using their own hands, just as they would with real objects.
